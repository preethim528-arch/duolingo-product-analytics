Learning Behavior & Premium Conversion on Duolingo
📊 Project Overview
This personal product analytics project explores how learner behavior influences premium conversion in a freemium product like Duolingo.
The goal is to identify high-intent free learners and understand where targeted conversion strategies can drive sustainable premium growth.

❓ Product Question
How can Duolingo identify high-intent free learners and convert them to premium at the right moment without disrupting the learning experience?

🔍 Analysis Focus
Learning consistency (daily streaks)
Engagement depth (lessons completed, session duration)
Behavioral differences between free and premium users
Identification of high-intent free users within the freemium funnel
Potential revenue opportunity from behavior-based targeting
💡 Key Insight
Learning consistency is the strongest signal of premium intent.
A subset of free learners already behaves like premium users, indicating a focused and realistic opportunity for targeted feature unlocks or nudges instead of broad discounts.

📈 Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard visually connects:

User behavior → Intent → Conversion opportunity → Revenue impact

It is designed to help product and growth teams quickly understand:

What differentiates premium users
Which free users are most likely to convert
Where premium growth opportunities exist
Screenshots of the dashboard are available in the /screenshots folder.

🛠 Tools Used
PostgreSQL – Data exploration and KPI validation
Power BI – Dashboard design and storytelling
DAX – Metrics, segmentation logic, and KPIs
⚠️ Data Note
The dataset used in this project is a sample dataset created for learning purposes.
Absolute values are illustrative, while insights focus on behavioral patterns and analytical approach rather than real-world performance.
