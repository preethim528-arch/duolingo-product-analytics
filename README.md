# Learning Behavior & Premium Conversion on Duolingo

A product analytics project exploring how learner behavior, engagement,
and learning consistency influence premium conversion in a freemium
language-learning product.

The analysis focuses on identifying high-intent free learners and
understanding where behavior-based conversion strategies could create
sustainable premium growth.

---

## Product Question

> How can Duolingo identify high-intent free learners and convert them
> to premium at the right moment without disrupting the learning
> experience?

---

## Business Objective

The objective is to understand behavioral differences between free and
premium users and identify signals that can help product and growth
teams prioritize high-intent free learners.

The analysis focuses on:

- Learning consistency
- Engagement depth
- Premium vs. free user behavior
- High-intent free-user identification
- Premium conversion opportunity
- Potential revenue opportunity

---

## Key Analytical Questions

1. Do premium users demonstrate stronger learning consistency?
2. How does daily streak behavior differ between free and premium users?
3. Which free learners demonstrate high-intent behavior?
4. What engagement patterns are associated with premium users?
5. Where could targeted conversion strategies be introduced?
6. How can product teams encourage conversion without disrupting the
   learning experience?

---

## Analysis Focus

### Learning Consistency

Analyzed daily streak behavior to understand whether consistent learning
is associated with premium usage.

### Engagement Depth

Examined learner engagement patterns including:

- Lessons completed
- Session duration
- Daily streaks
- Learning consistency

### User Segmentation

Compared:

- Premium users
- Free users
- High-intent free users

The objective was to identify free learners whose behavior already
resembles that of premium users.

---

## Key Insight

**Learning consistency emerged as the strongest signal of premium intent
within this analysis.**

A subset of free learners already demonstrates behavior similar to
premium users, suggesting an opportunity for targeted conversion
strategies rather than broad discounting.

The analytical opportunity can be summarized as:

**Consistent Learning → High Intent → Timely Intervention → Premium
Conversion Opportunity**

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard connects:

**User Behavior → Intent → Conversion Opportunity → Revenue Impact**

The dashboard is designed to help product and growth teams understand:
